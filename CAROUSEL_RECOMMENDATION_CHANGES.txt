CAROUSEL AND RECOMMENDATION CHANGES

1. Featured Products now moves automatically every 5 seconds.
2. The carousel moves two cards at a time, so more cards create more movement positions.
3. The carousel loops back to the beginning after reaching the end.
4. The arrows still work manually.
5. Dragging with a mouse or touch still works.
6. Automatic movement pauses while the user is using or hovering over the carousel.
7. Small position dots are created automatically based on the number of cards.
8. Recommended Products can now show up to 12 products instead of only 6.
9. Recommendations use recent searches, searched categories, viewed products, ratings, and newer products when there is not enough history.
10. The recommendation message now clearly explains that it is based on the user's recent activity.
